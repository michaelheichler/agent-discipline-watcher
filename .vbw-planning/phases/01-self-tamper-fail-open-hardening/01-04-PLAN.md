- Only render_text and render_md are exposed. render_json escapes by construction through json.dumps and must not change.
- Today the sole escaping is one backtick replace on excerpt inside _markdown_rows. path and hint are interpolated raw, and no field is stripped of control characters or newlines.
- Excerpts and hints originate from scanned file content, a trust boundary: treat them as hostile input per the security skill (output encoding rule).
- payloads.py uses operator.is_(type(value), T) at 12 sites (lines 41, 45, 52, 57, 65, 87, 89, 91, 172, 205, 209, 224). hooks/failure.py lines 81-82 duplicate the idiom as _has_exact_type. Simplify both, keep behavior identical (exact type match, bool must not pass as int).
