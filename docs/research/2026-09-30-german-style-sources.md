# German style sources for ADW, found in local libraries

Scope. Read-only search of the library MCP, /Volumes/library, and /Users/nootnoot/Documents/eBooks. No Wolf Schneider, Ludwig Reiners, or Bastian Sick title exists in either location. No book implements a German readability formula (Wiener Sachtextformel, LIX, or the Hamburger Verständlichkeitsmodell by Langer, Schulz von Thun, Tausch). That gap stays open. A sentence length norm for German still needs an external source or a hand built formula.

## Ranked hits

### 1. Einfach können. Rechtschreibung, Zeichensetzung und Grammatik
Dudenredaktion, 2025, EPUB, `/Volumes/library/books/Einfach können - Rechtschreibung, Zeichensetzung und - Dudenredaktion (2025).epub`.

The table of contents covers every German punctuation mark (comma, period, exclamation point, question mark, semicolon, colon, dash, parentheses, quotation marks, apostrophe). It also covers grammar, including the simple sentence and the compound sentence, which is Schachtelsatz territory. This is the strongest single source for the ADW needs "German typography and punctuation" and "Schachtelsatz patterns."

We can derive a rule list without quoting. The content is grammar fact (when German punctuation rules demand a comma, what a Gedankenstrich marks), not Duden's literary expression. Restate each rule in our own words.

### 2. Kann man sagen, muss man aber nicht
Andreas Neuenkirchen, Duden, 2023, MOBI, `/Volumes/library/_unsupported/Kann man sagen, muss man aber nicht_ Die größten -- Andreas Neuenkirchen -- 2023 -- Duden -- ... .mobi`.

It recovers fragments that point to Floskeldeutsch (filler corporate speak), Bürokratensprache, and common grammar slip ups. This fits the ADW need "filler and Floskeln" directly. A tool such as Calibre opens a real table of contents.

We can derive a rule list without quoting. It catalogs common mistakes and clichés, a fact pattern, not the author's prose.

### 3. Duden Business Bullshit. Managerdeutsch in 100 Blasen und Phrasen
Jens Bergmann, Dudenverlag, 2021, PDF, `/Volumes/library/books/Duden Business Bullshit Managerdeutsch in 100 Blasen und - Jens Bergmann (2021).pdf`.

The table of contents lists Imponiervokabular, Beschwörungsformeln, Euphemismen, Gutfirmensprech, and Psychotalk. The last chapter, "Nullnachrichten und Sprachunfälle," is a catalog of empty filler phrases. That fits the ADW need "Floskeln and filler" directly.

We can derive a rule list without quoting. The catalogued phrases are common jargon, not copyrightable text. Write our own commentary and our own groupings.

### 4. Deutsch für Architekten und Bauingenieure
Felix Friedrich, Sharon Heidenreich, Springer Vieweg, 2025 (2nd edition), PDF, `/Volumes/library/books/Deutsch für Architekten und Bauingenieure Ein - Felix Friedrich, Sharon Heidenreich (2025).pdf`.

A German for Fachsprache textbook. Chapter 9 teaches the Vorgangspassiv (process passive) for describing building procedures, and several chapters teach Nomen-Verb-Verbindungen (light verb constructions, a Nominalstil marker). Useful as a reference for what correct Passiv and Nominalstil look like grammatically, not for arguing against them. Serves the ADW need "Passiv and Nominalstil patterns" only as a grammar reference, moderate usefulness.

We can derive a rule list without quoting, since the target content is grammar description.

### 5. Deutsch intensiv, Schreiben B2 and Schreiben C1
Sandra Hohmann and Dorothea Stein-Bassler, Klett Sprachen, PDFs, `/Volumes/library/books/Deutsch intensiv B2 [ ] Schreiben berufsbezogene Themen - Sandra Hohmann; Ernst Klett Verlag (Stuttgart).pdf` and `/Volumes/library/books/Deutsch intensiv C1 [ ] Schreiben Dorothea - Dorothea Stein - Bassler Arwen Schnack (2020).pdf`.

Writing workbooks for B2 and C1 exam candidates. They teach text structure, argumentation, and Redemittel (set phrases), aimed at non-native learners passing an exam, not at cutting Floskeln or Passiv from professional prose. Weak fit for any ADW need. Keep as a low priority fallback only.

### 6. Digital erfolgreich kommunizieren
Ingrid Stephan, Duden, 2021, AZW3, `/Volumes/library/_unsupported/Digital erfolgreich kommunizieren -- Ingrid Stephan -- 2021 -- Duden -- ... .azw3`.

A strings pass shows email and Outlook checklists, subject line and structure advice. This is workplace communication etiquette, not sentence level prose style. Weak fit for the ADW needs. Low priority.

### 7. Duden, Das Fremdwörterbuch (Band 5)
Hans Jürgen Heringer, Dudenverlag, 13th edition, 2023, PDF, `/Volumes/library/books/Duden - Das Fremdwörterbuch - Dr Hans Jürgen Heringer (2023).pdf`.

A loanword dictionary. Useful only to define or flag an Anglicism or a foreign word ADW marks as jargon, not as a source of prose rules. Reference tier, not a style guide.

### 8. Duden, Deutsches Universalwörterbuch
Dudenredaktion, PDF, `/Volumes/library/books/Duden - Deutsches Universalwörterbuch Das große - Bibliographisches Institut Dudenredaktion.pdf`.

A general dictionary, 11,953 chunks in the library index. Reference only, not a style source.

## Excluded as off topic
- Kaputte Wörter? Vom Umgang mit heikler Sprache (Matthias Heine, Duden, 2022) covers sensitive and loaded vocabulary (colonial and racist terms), a different concern than clarity or Floskeln.
- Antisemitismus in der Sprache (Ronen Steinke, 2021) treats word choice in a political and ethical sense, not prose craft.

## What is still missing
The library holds no Wolf Schneider, no Ludwig Reiners, and no Bastian Sick title. It holds no Langer, Schulz von Thun, or Tausch Verständlichkeitsmodell either. Beyond the punctuation chapter in "Einfach können," no German typography manual exists here. A German readability measure and a sentence length norm both need a source outside this library scan. Buy one of the authors above, or build the measure from the Duden grammar rules already found.
